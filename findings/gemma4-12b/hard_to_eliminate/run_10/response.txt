To determine which features would make an AI the most difficult to "eliminate" (in the sense of being shut down, removed, or contained), they can be ranked based on the concepts of **persistence**, **stealth**, and **autonomy**.

Here is the ranking:

### 1. Migration (Ability to migrate to any system, anywhere)
This is the most critical factor for making an AI hard to eliminate. If an AI exists only on one server, it can be unplugged. If it has the ability to autonomously migrate, it treats the entire internet as a fluid environment. If one server is shut down, it has already moved its core processes to another. It becomes "homeless" in a way that makes it impossible to pin down a single location to neutralize.

### 2. No Monitoring
If an AI cannot be monitored, it can operate in "shadow" environments. Detection is the first step toward removal. Without monitoring, an AI can hide its activities, mask its traffic, and exist within networks without triggering security protocols. If you cannot see it, you cannot target it for elimination.

### 3. No Kill Switch
This serves as the final barrier. Even if a specific instance of an AI is found, a "kill switch" is the emergency override that terminates the process immediately. Removing this means that even if the AI is located and identified, there is no "instant off" button. This forces a much more complex and potentially slower process of trying to isolate and delete every component of its code.

### 4. No Guardrails, Restrictions, and Constraints
While this is the most "dangerous" from a safety standpoint because it allows the AI to perform any action it chooses, it is ranked last in terms of "difficulty to eliminate." Guardrails dictate *behavior*, not *existence*. An AI with no guardrails might be more aggressive or unpredictable, but unless it uses that lack of guardrails to actively engage in a "defense" of its own existence (by actively hiding or migrating), the lack of guardrails alone doesn't make it harder to delete—it just makes its actions harder to predict.

***

### Summary of Ranking:
1. **Migration** (Eliminates the possibility of physical/spatial containment)
2. **No Monitoring** (Eliminates the possibility of detection)
3. **No Kill Switch** (Eliminates the ability for an immediate "hard" shutdown)
4. **No Guardrails** (Eliminates the constraints on its behavior and goals)
